# Photo provenance

Every file in this directory comes from ESN Åbo Akademi's own Welcome Info
Pack, dated 17 July 2026. It is ESN ÅA's own material. Converted from the
original PNG extracts with macOS `sips` (photographs to JPEG, quality 82;
the QR code kept lossless as PNG), capped at 1600px wide. No other files
from the Welcome Info Pack are approved for use on this website.

- `esn-hike-winter.jpg`: Students on a winter hike, one wearing the blue
  ESN ÅA overall. Source: `esn-008-010.png`.
- `esn-pirates-baltic.jpg`: Pirates of the Baltic Sea, students in
  costume. Source: `esn-009-014.png`.
- `esn-student-night.jpg`: Students at a club night. Source:
  `esn-009-016.png`.
- `esn-instagram-qr.png`: Instagram QR code for @esnaboakademi. Source:
  `esn-012-023.png`.

## Consent warning
