# Office-walk clip, Wave 1

Purpose: show a new student exactly how to find the ESN Åbo Akademi office, so
they can replay it on the walk over. It is a utility clip, not a promotional
film. The cinematic film is Wave 3 and is a separate brief.

## Constraints

- Real footage only. No generated scenery. Higgsfield may be used afterwards
  for stabilisation, cleanup, reformatting and original audio only.
- **No dates, prices or opening hours in the edit.** Those change, and video is
  the hardest asset to update. The clip ends on one call to action:
  esnabo.org/arriving.
- Shoot when the office is quiet, so no identifiable third parties appear and
  no consent forms are needed. If anyone recognisable ends up in frame, either
  reshoot or collect a written release.
- Keep essential content inside the vertical safe zone if a 9:16 cutdown is
  made, between y=270 and y=1536 on a 1080 by 1920 frame.

## Target

About 60 seconds. Phone camera is fine. Shoot horizontal, framed so a 9:16
crop still works. Steady, walking pace, no narration needed.

## Shot list

1. Turku Cathedral, wide, establishing. The cathedral is the landmark everyone
   can already find.
2. Turn from the cathedral toward Tuomiokirkontori. Show the direction of
   travel clearly.
3. The Geologicum building exterior, wide enough to recognise from across the
   square, then a closer shot of the entrance.
4. The main door. Hold on it. This is where the grey Åbo Akademi key is needed,
   which is the single most confusing step.
5. Stairs to the second floor.
6. The office door itself, with the ESN signage if there is any.
7. Optional closing shot: a board member opening the door and waving.

## On-screen text

Rendered locally in the deterministic workflow, not burned in by any AI tool.
Oswald display face, official ESN colours.

- "Start at the cathedral"
- "Cross to Tuomiokirkontori"
- "This is Geologicum"
- "You need your grey ÅA key for this door"
- "Second floor"
- "That's us"
- End card: "esnabo.org/arriving"
