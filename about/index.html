What ESN does

The Erasmus Student Network was founded in 1989 and is one of the biggest student associations in Europe, active on local, national, and international levels, with around 15,000 active members supporting international students through buddy sections like ours.

Part of a bigger story

How the Erasmus Student Network began

ESN's History Book tells how a small group of former Erasmus students turned one practical idea into an international volunteer network: help future exchange students make the most of their experience. ESN Åbo Akademi carries that idea forward here in Turku (Åbo).

1987

Erasmus opens new doors

The European Community approved the Erasmus programme, giving more students the opportunity to study abroad.

1989

Students helping students

At an evaluation meeting in Ghent, 32 former Erasmus students identified the practical problems exchange students faced. Their answer became ESN's guiding idea: students helping students.

16 Oct 1989

The first ESN section

The first section to use the name Erasmus Student Network was founded in Utrecht, the Netherlands.

1990

ESN International is founded

Representatives met in Copenhagen to establish ESN International as a legal association, connecting the growing network across Europe.

ESN Åbo Akademi

ESN vid Åbo Akademi r.f. is the official ESN section at Åbo Akademi University, the only Swedish-speaking multidisciplinary university in Finland. Together with ESN Uni Turku, it's one of two ESN sections active in Turku (Åbo).

We help exchange students get enjoyable cultural and social experiences while they're here, foster inclusivity in student circles, and help exchange students in any way we can.

And here's the part we're proudest of: ESN ÅA is a non-profit association run entirely by volunteers. Nobody is paid — every trip, sitz, and sauna night is organised by students who do it for the love of it.
